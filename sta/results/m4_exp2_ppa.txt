M4 EXP2 — Arithmetic Selection Optimization
=============================================

Technology:
  Sky130 HD
  TT corner

  1.80V

Clock constraint:
  Period: 10.000 ns

------------------------------------------------------------
STA NETLIST PREPARATION
------------------------------------------------------------

The synthesis-generated Sky130 netlists contained 1-bit
generic $mux cells corresponding to reset muxing.

These were converted structurally to:
  $mux -> sky130_fd_sc_hd__mux2_1

The conversion was performed separately for baseline and EXP2
using:

  sta/scripts/convert_baseline_sta_muxes.py
  sta/scripts/convert_exp2_sta_muxes.py

Baseline generic muxes converted:
  1528

EXP2 generic muxes converted:
  1528

The final STA netlists contain no generic $mux, $dff, or
always constructs.

------------------------------------------------------------

  sky130_fd_sc_hd__tt_025C_1v80.lib

  Total area = sum(cell_count × Liberty_cell_area)

EXP2:

------------------------------------------------------------
EXP2 CONCLUSION
------------------------------------------------------------

EXP2 unified ADD/SUB/ADDI arithmetic selection in the ALU.

Compared with baseline, EXP2 achieved:

  Mapped cell area:       -0.143%
  Mapped cell count:      -1.08%
  Worst setup slack:      +2.493 ns
  Critical arrival time:  -2.493 ns
  Estimated Fmax:         +16.8%
  Hold slack:             unchanged

The 10 ns timing target is still violated.

The remaining critical path contains a long chain of
sky130_fd_sc_hd__maj3_1 cells associated with the 32-bit
arithmetic/carry path.
